Essential Piano & Music Theory

1. The Musical Alphabet

Western music uses 7 basic note names: A, B, C, D, E, F, G. On a piano, these correspond to the white keys, repeating in cycles called octaves.

2. Correct Fingering

Proper finger placement (numbered 1 for thumb to 5 for pinky) ensures fluid motion, high speed, and prevents wrist strain during complex passages.

3. Chords & Harmony

Playing three or more notes simultaneously (a triad, e.g., C-E-G) forms a chord, creating rich harmonic backing for melodies.

4. Octaves & Frequency

Moving an octave higher doubles the frequency (Hz) of the sound wave, producing a higher pitch while retaining the exact same musical character.
